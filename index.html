Extra Considerations

  • Cut Depth 2X Tool Diameter: Reduce chipload by 20-25% to account for increased cutting forces.
  • Cut Depth 3X Tool Diameter: Reduce chipload by 40-50% for even deeper cuts to maintain tool integrity and finish quality.
  • Consider the rigidity of your machine and fixturing. Less rigid setups may require a lower chip load.
  • Material hardness and abrasiveness can affect optimal chip load. Harder or more abrasive materials often require a lower chip load.
  • Tool material and coating play a significant role. High-performance coatings can allow for higher chip loads.
  • Listen to the machine. Abnormal noises can indicate too high or too low a chip load.
  • Always start with recommended values and adjust based on real-world results and tool wear.
